# Project & Data Limitations

1. **Observational Selection Effects**:
   - Radio pulsars are detected via beamed radiation towards Earth; unbeamed pulsars are missed.
   - Flux limitations favor nearby or high-luminosity objects.
   - High Dispersion Measure (DM) along the Galactic plane reduces sensitivity to short-period pulsars.

2. **Incompleteness**:
   - Mass and radius measurements require special conditions (e.g. relativistic binary timing or X-ray spectroscopy) and exist for only a small fraction of known objects.

3. **Intrinsic vs. Observed Population**:
   - The observed population is a selection-biased sample of the true Galactic neutron star population.
